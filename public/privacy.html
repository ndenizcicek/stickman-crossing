Stickman Crossing Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Stickman Crossing ("the game") is made by Burak Çiçek, an independent developer. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your best score for each city, the last city you chose and your sound setting. This data stays on your device. It is never sent to us, and deleting the game deletes it.

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by ads shown through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (only if you allow tracking when the app asks),
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device type, operating system and app version,
  • which ads were shown and whether they were clicked.

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for your permission before any tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads. You can change these choices at any time in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
